Smart Culinary Substitutions

Need to replace a dangerous allergen without ruining the dish? Gemma suggests 1:1 culinary substitutes that preserve texture, moisture, and umami.

Shared Kitchen & Roommate Cross-Contamination Protocols

When living with a friend who has Celiac disease or anaphylactic allergies, simple kitchen habits can be life-saving.

💡
Golden Rule of Cross-Contact Prevention:

"Sanitizing" or "heating" does not destroy food allergen proteins. Gluten and nut proteins are resilient heat-stable compounds. Physical washing with soap, warm water, and clean friction is required to remove allergen residue completely.
